# Original Aster Valley scenery models

Generated from the project-authored `content/scenery/tools/models.mjs`. These eight original parametric assets are dedicated to landmark/town scenery and are released for use in this game under CC0-1.0. No third-party geometry or texture was used. The existing Art Agent `assets/curated/` models remain read-only; model upgrades should come from licensed, source-controlled art.

These are deliberately **stylized low-poly starter silhouettes**, not photorealistic architectural assets. Triangles and glTF surface counts are reported by `node content/scenery/tools/models.mjs --check`.
