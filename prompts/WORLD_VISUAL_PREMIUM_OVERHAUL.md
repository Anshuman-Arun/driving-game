Use authoritative current road geometry, including widened roads, shoulders, junction polygons, bridge approaches/decks, gravel lanes, shortcuts, garage/spawn/reset areas and race starts. Reserve conservative clearances beyond edges; keep tall trees, poles, buildings and rocks outside safety envelopes, maintain sightlines at junctions/hairpins. Preserve intentional roadside barriers outside drivable width. No changing road graph merely to hide problems.

## 3. Materials, terrain and texture repair
Audit all actual live mesh/material bindings: missing/pink/black textures, UV seams/stretching/repetition, UV scale in meters, correct normal orientation and strength, sRGB albedo versus linear roughness/metallic/AO/normal maps, channel packing, alpha mode, mipmaps, anisotropy, VRAM compression, shader parameter names and import references. Check pavement at curves/junctions/bridges, terrain blend masks/road shoulders, grass/gravel/rock/sand/water, car materials, buildings and props. Use appropriate PBR textures from existing assets/forza_env and approved premium assets, world-space/triplanar mapping where suitable, subtle macro variation and distance attenuation; avoid giant repeating tiles, excessive gloss, harsh color mismatch, stretched road markings, z-fighting and flickering. Check render and collision alignment, road-to-ground joins, slopes and water levels. No indiscriminate shader/material replacement or arbitrary saturation boosts. Capture close, medium and distant views of every material type. Validate each quality preset.
