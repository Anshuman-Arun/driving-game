## Objective and reference
Aim for the visual richness of Forza Horizon 6, not a copy of its assets, map or Japan branding. Preserve Aster Valley's original temperate mountain/countryside/town/lake design, 2 x 2 km existing road graph, bridge collision, physics, HUD, offline operation and 3 fictional cars. The reference has lush layered forests, dappled light, brighter neutral gray asphalt, irregular natural shoulders, realistic roadside objects, coherent materials and a lower chase camera. Our current screenshot has sparse grass tufts on green carpet, repetitive umbrella trees, broad blank terrain, dark cracked asphalt and weak contact shading. Achieve quality through real asset meshes and composition, not saturation or bloom alone. No new graphics engine or paid assets.
