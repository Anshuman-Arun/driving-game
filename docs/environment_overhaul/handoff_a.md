# Agent A — procedural ground cover handoff

Base: 67023539e56e5e80d5efcd3f3239be9650c6f462, branch feat/env-a-ground-cover. Wave 1 isolated implementation; no production wiring, canonical data edits, or external art. All new assets are original procedural geometry and shader code.

## Integration
Attach a Node3D running res://modules/ground_cover/ground_cover.gd to an identity-transform world-aligned parent. After world runtime builds its real clearance index, call:

```gdscript
var cover = preload("res://modules/ground_cover/ground_cover.gd").new()
add_child(cover)
var ok: bool = cover.build(world, "medium", "res://content/scenery/environment_overhaul/masks", {
    "pilot_bounds": Rect2(-800, 250, 300, 300),
    "seed": 20261125
})
# Every frame after camera update:
cover.update_focus(camera.global_position, vehicle_velocity)
# Optional; disabled initially:
cover.set_vehicle_interaction(vehicle.global_position, vehicle_velocity, false)
# Settings change:
cover.set_graphics("low")
# Session exit:
cover.shutdown()
```

Check build result and get_stats().state/fallback. Keep legacy cover until coordinated A/B replacement; don't stack both. Mask missing/corrupt, unavailable world API, clearance index empty, replaced or triangle count changed, nonidentity transform: fail closed. Production builds with a known partial-preview runtime report are disabled; the full roadworks smoke explicitly uses the debug fixture sampler for independent clearance tests. Fixture sampler is explicit debug-only. Mask uses center-aligned 2 m RGBA cells with SHA-256 verification. Real surface height sampled per tuft with slope rejection; every accepted candidate uses production triangle-index overlaps with shoulders enabled and conservative swept bounds of all LOD meshes, including 0.24 m maximum shader displacement and 0.12 m margin. The real clearance API deliberately permits ground beneath high bridge decks.

## Local starting values (not accepted performance)
| Preset | Near/mid/far radii (m) | Max candidate density (tufts/m²) | Active cap | Candidates/frame | Chunk uploads/frame | Chunk cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 20/45/80 | 4.8/1.92/0.56 | 18,000 | 1200 | 2 | 120 |
| Medium | 25/65/130 | 8.8/3.2/0.88 | 54,000 | 2400 | 3 | 300 |
| High | 32/80/150 | 13.6/4.8/1.28 | 90,000 | 3600 | 4 | 360 |

16 m cells, 64x64 jittered candidate lattice, keyed Feistel permutation, rank-prefix stable subsets, biome acceptance and slope filter. Meshes are 8 curved four-segment blades near, 5 two-segment mid, 3 triangular far; five original variants. Height bases 0.15/0.24/0.39/0.27/0.20 m, widths 0.012–0.018 m; tune in grass_mesh.gd. Per-tuft scale 0.78–1.20, yaw, terrain-normal orientation. Chunk culling uses conservative custom AABB; shadows off. Predictive prefetch 0.65 s, including detail prewarming at predicted distance +12 m; adaptive LOD hysteresis at most 5 m and at most 40% of the smallest tier spacing. Node pool retains at most 75 empty batch nodes, not old instance buffers. Hard instance and job caps; one chunk may use up to five MultiMeshes. Budget allocation reserves one candidate per selected chunk, skips zero-density tiers, reserves 50% near, 32% mid and 18% far of remaining candidate and chunk limits, then redistributes unused capacity. Per-chunk quotas quantized to 32 ranks at or above 64 and monotonically clamped across distance tiers. Consequently realized density may be lower than the candidate maxima in the table, especially at far range. Outer distance and surplus candidate ranks use stable opaque whole-tuft discard; extra blades shrink about their own root anchors before LOD boundaries using the encoded chunk center and stable blade ID. This is single-batch geometric/density fade; no simultaneous dual-batch geometry crossfade or extra instance reservation. Internal LOD also uses hysteresis and nested density. Old batches are retained if accepted count fits the new quota; otherwise retired before regeneration to preserve hard caps. In-flight jobs keep their deterministic prefix when detail or quota changes. Brief gaps are still possible. Changed preset rebuilds rather than blending; repeating an identical preset is now a no-op. No whole-world dense cache, per-blade physics, threads or save files.

Tuning dictionary: density (three descending fractions of 16 candidates/m², max 1), radii (three ascending metres), cap/checks/uploads/chunks/upload_instances/work_usec (hard maximum High limits), wind_enabled, wind_direction (Vector2), wind_strength (0–0.16 m), fade_enabled, lod_blade_fade_enabled, lod_density_fade_enabled, plain_material (build-time diagnostic StandardMaterial3D, no wind/fade). Wind and vehicle bend combined clamped to 0.24 m, anchored at UV.y=0. Vehicle interaction is opt-in. Start with 0.25/0.10/0.03 density on lower hardware, then sweep default and higher near density while collecting frame-time and visual data. No transparent cards; no external assets. Stats report cumulative CPU wall time in microseconds including upload, separate upload time, candidate count, rejects, active and queued chunks, per-LOD instances, estimated triangles and bytes (64 bytes per transform/custom data, mesh arrays estimate, excludes driver/allocator overhead). Chunk upload count is not a count of individual species buffers. Actual per-frame upload instance caps are 1800/2800/4200. Soft CPU work budgets are 1800/2200/3000 microseconds; timers are checked every 32 candidates, and a single chunk upload can exceed the soft budget. Planning time and cancelled/retargeted jobs are reported separately. No CPU or GPU engine benchmark measured yet.
